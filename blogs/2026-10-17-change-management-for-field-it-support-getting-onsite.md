Quick answer: Field IT support belongs in change management, but it shouldn’t be slowed by it. Field work fits into change management by classifying onsite tasks as standard, normal, or emergency changes. Routine, low-risk tasks with proven runbooks become pre-approved standard changes that don’t need a change advisory board (CAB) meeting.
